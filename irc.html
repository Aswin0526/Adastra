INTERNATIONAL ROVER CHALLENGE - 2025

What is IRC?

The International Rover Challenge (IRC) is a global platform that brings together university students to design, build, and operate innovative rovers capable of tackling complex, simulated space exploration challenges. The competition fosters creativity, teamwork, and technical expertise while addressing real-world issues in robotics and planetary science.

About SPROS (Space Robotics Society)

The Space Robotics Society (SPROS) is the visionary organization behind IRC. It is committed to advancing the frontiers of space robotics and automation, providing students and researchers with opportunities to explore cutting-edge technologies. Through IRC and other initiatives, SPROS nurtures talent that will shape the future of space exploration.

Competition Overview

IRC features four missions that simulate real-world extraterrestrial exploration tasks, testing teams on their engineering, programming, and problem-solving skills. The competition challenges participants to innovate and adapt their rover designs to meet rigorous technical and operational demands, reflecting scenarios faced by space exploration missions.

Missions and Tasks

IRC features four distinct missions: Astrobiology Expedition (ABEx), Reconnaissance & Delivery Operation (RDO), Autonomous Expedition (AutEx), and Instrument Deployment & Maintenance Operation (IDMO). Each mission tests a different aspect of rover capability, from autonomous navigation and environmental analysis to equipment servicing and delivery operations. These missions push the boundaries of innovation and require rovers to operate with high precision and resilience under simulated extraterrestrial conditions.

Key Areas: PIMA and BPP

PIMA (Project Implementation and Management Assessment) evaluates how well teams prepare and manage their projects, including design documentation, resource allocation, and strategy implementation. BPP (Business and Partnership Planning) focuses on assessing the team's ability to secure resources, form partnerships, and present viable business strategies for their rover project. These areas highlight the importance of project management and entrepreneurial skills in engineering challenges.
